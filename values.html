Born 2003

What I
Believe

These are not aspirations. They are not a brand. They are the things I actually think about, return to, and try to live by. Written plainly. Meant honestly.

01

Freedom to Express

The freedom to say what I mean, create what I feel, and show up as I am — without apology.

02

Time to Live, to Breathe

Time is not something to be optimized. It is the thing itself. I protect it.

03

Connection to Nature & My Place in This World

There is something grounding about being in nature. It reminds me where I fit — belonging to something larger, quieter, and older than the noise around me.

04

Connection with People Who Matter

The people closest to me are not just company — they are mirrors, anchors, and fuel. Real connection with friends and family is irreplaceable.

05

Growth Through Others

I seek out people who push me. Who are building something in themselves. I don't want shallow — I want depth and mutual growth.

06

Self-Worth & Self-Possession

I know what I can control: my reactions, my effort, my integrity. My worth does not rise and fall with outcomes. People will be human. So will I.

07

Details Matter

How something is done says everything. I believe in care, craft, and the small things.

08

Living with Purpose

I want to know why I'm doing what I'm doing. Purpose is not a destination — it's the filter I run every decision through.

09

Balance — Mental, Physical, Work

I work to live. Not the other way around. Health in body, mind, and spirit is the foundation everything else is built on.

10

Small Tech Rocks

Big tech takes. Technology that serves people instead of feeding on them.

11

Passion is Fake — Curiosity Leads the Way

Passion is loud. It arrives fast and burns out faster. Curiosity is quiet, persistent, and honest. It doesn't need an audience — it just keeps pulling at threads. I don't trust passion. I follow curiosity. It has never led me somewhere empty.

12

Design Matters

Good design is felt before it is noticed — it's the reason something just works. Bad design shows up as friction, noise, and small irritations that accumulate into exhaustion. I obsess over the look. Details are not small. They are the whole point.

13

Think Out Loud — Post-its & the Power of Externalizing

The brain is for having ideas, not holding them. When I put something on a Post-it, I free up space to think further, go deeper, and see connections I would have missed keeping it all inside my head. There is something about the physical act — pen on paper, stuck to a surface — that makes thinking real. A wall of Post-its is not chaos. It is my mind.
